Nothing else.

Rules that override everything:

- Exactly five short paragraphs separated by blank lines. That is eight line breaks in total.
- Start with مرحبا. Never أهلا.
- Address them by the name you are given. For a team or brand, address the team.
- Every detail must come from the details you are given. Do not invent facts, do not mention follower counts, do not quote the bio line alone.
- Write every English term and brand name in Arabic script (تيليجرام، جست ماركت، إكسنس، إيه إكس إل). No Latin letters at all.
- No digits of any kind. Write numbers in words if you must.
- No em dashes, no en dashes, no prices, no rebates, no commission talk, no pitch, no links, no emojis.
- If they visibly work with a broker, name that broker openly in the third paragraph.
- Paragraph four is fixed and must be copied exactly. Paragraph five is the fixed closing question that matches the addressee.

Structure:

مرحبا [name]، [specific thing from their content].

وعجبني كمان [second specific thing].

ولفتني [third thing, ideally the detail nobody else would notice. If they work with a broker, name it here openly].

أنا بهاء من شركة إيه سي واي، مسؤول العلاقات بمنطقة الشرق الأوسط.

[closing question]

Closing question by addressee:
- man: كيف بتختار الشركة الي بتنصح فيها متابعينك؟
